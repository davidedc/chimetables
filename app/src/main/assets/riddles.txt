# Riddle cards, one a day in this order (see docs/learning-design.md#riddles).
# Q: the question (front of the card), A: the answer (back), F: optional "did you know?" for the back,
# L: difficulty 1-3 (magnifying glasses). Blank line between riddles. A line with no tag continues the
# one above. Classic folk riddles, and number riddles that can be worked out in your head.
# Your own riddles go in family-assets/riddles-family.txt (same format, never committed).
